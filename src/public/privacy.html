VariantScope privacy policy

Published by Reeper Studio. Last updated 5 November 2026.

Short version

VariantScope has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it has no server of its own. Reeper Studio does not receive or collect any information about you.

What the app sends, and to whom

When you search a gene, the app sends the gene symbol you typed (for example TP53) straight from your device to these public databases to download results:

  • UniProt (rest.uniprot.org)
  • AlphaFold Protein Structure Database (alphafold.ebi.ac.uk)
  • NCBI, for ClinVar and Gene (eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov)
  • gnomAD (gnomad.broadinstitute.org)

Like any website or app you connect to, these services can see your IP address and the request. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it. VariantScope sends them nothing else: no name, no email, no device identifiers.

What is stored on your device

Genes you search are saved on your own device so they open again without internet (and, in the desktop app, so you can put them in folders). Nothing is uploaded. In the desktop app you can delete saved genes from the home page; in the web version you can clear the site's data in your browser.

What this app is not

VariantScope is for education only. It is not medical advice and must not be used to diagnose, treat or decide anything about health. We do not collect health information about you: the genes you look up are not linked to you and are not sent to Reeper Studio.

Children

The app does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. To ask a question, open an issue at github.com/reeper316099/VariantScope/issues.
