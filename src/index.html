      About VariantScope

      VariantScope combines the AlphaFold structure of a human protein with ClinVar variants and gnomAD population frequencies. Educational use only. Not medical advice.

      Limitations

      • AlphaFold predicts structure. A prediction is not experimental proof, and low pLDDT regions are uncertain.
      • ClinVar review quality varies. Check the review status, and note that submitters can disagree.
      • VUS means “variant of uncertain significance”: it is not evidence for or against disease.
      • Residue numbers come from the UniProt reference sequence. ClinVar and gnomAD use a specific transcript (often MANE Select), so numbering can differ for some genes. VariantScope checks every variant and does not plot ones that do not match.
      • Only single-residue missense changes are shown. Frameshift, nonsense, splice, in-frame and non-coding records are counted but not plotted.
      • For large genes only a limited number of variants are loaded (pathogenic and benign first).
      • gnomAD reflects the people sampled, not the whole world, and is not a disease-free control group.
